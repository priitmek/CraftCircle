# CraftCircle changelog

## 1.7.0

- **Crafter directory.** Browse by profession or by person, then open the crafter's recipe catalog.
- **Cached recipes.** Browse and search previously opened catalogs while crafters are offline. Online refreshes can confirm unchanged catalogs with a short reply.
- **Sync improvements.** Recipe actions get priority, catalog transfers show progress, and equipment changes send only gear data.
- **Can help.** Filter the request board to items you already have in your bags. Inventory counts stay on your client.

## 1.6.1

- **Item link entry.** The request field accepts full item links, raw `item:` strings, numeric IDs, and items dragged from bags. Invalid or unrelated links leave the focused field alone when link-clicking.

## 1.6.0

- **Offline sync.** Previously synced guildmates and Battle.net friends stay visible in **My Circle** as **Offline · Cached**. Requests and profession summaries remain available for up to 14 days. Full recipe catalogs still require the crafter to be online.
- **BoE Gear tab.** See unbound bind-on-equip gear in your bags and select an item to see who in your circle might benefit and by how many item levels. Offline matches use equipment synced in the last seven days and are marked **cached**.

## 1.5.0

- **BoE upgrade tooltips.** Hover over bind-on-equip gear to see potential item-level upgrades for guildmates and Battle.net friends with synced equipment.

## 1.4.0

- **Recipe search.** Find a recipe by name or ID across online crafters in your circle.
- **Clearer icons.** Recipe rows show the crafted item's icon when available. Contact rows use a sync indicator.
